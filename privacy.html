Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy describes how Gange Bookkeeping ("we") handles information accessed through its QuickBooks Online connection (the "App"). The App is a private tool used only by Gange Bookkeeping to provide bookkeeping services to its clients.

What we access

With your authorization through Intuit, the App reads and, where needed for our bookkeeping work, updates accounting data in your QuickBooks Online company. This can include company information, accounts, customers, vendors, transactions, invoices, bills, payments and reports. We access only the company you authorize.

How we use it

We use this data only to perform the bookkeeping services you engaged us for. We do not sell it, rent it, use it for advertising, or share it with other clients.

Where it goes

  • Sign-in credentials. The access tokens Intuit issues are stored only on Gange Bookkeeping's own computer, which is password-protected and encrypted.
  • AI assistant. We use Anthropic's Claude AI assistant to help review and organize records. When we do, the relevant QuickBooks data is sent to Anthropic to process that request, under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. We have opted out of allowing our conversations to be used to train Anthropic's models.
  • Working files. Reports or exports we prepare for you are kept in your client folder on our computer and shared only with you or people you direct us to.

We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention

We keep working files for as long as we provide services to you and as required for business records. When you disconnect the App, we delete its stored credentials for your company. You can ask us to delete working files at any time, subject to any legal record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps → Connected apps). You can contact us with questions or requests about your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above.
