Gange Bookkeeping QuickBooks connection

Gange Bookkeeping uses this private connection to work in its clients' QuickBooks Online companies: reviewing transactions, preparing reports and keeping the books current. It is used only by Gange Bookkeeping, for clients who have engaged it for bookkeeping services. It is not offered to the public.

Connecting

Gange Bookkeeping starts the connection from its own workstation and signs in to QuickBooks Online with Intuit. Each connection is limited to one client company. Nothing needs to be installed on your side.

Disconnecting

You can end access at any time. In QuickBooks Online, go to Settings → Apps → Connected apps (or your Intuit account's app connections) and disconnect Gange Bookkeeping. Access stops right away. You can also ask us to disconnect for you.

Reconnecting

If a connection was ended and you want us to resume work, contact us and we will start a new sign-in.
